# Screenshot shot list

Working document. Not shipped in the addon zip.

Ten shots for the CurseForge page, in the order they should appear there.
The first image is the one most people ever look at, so it is the one worth
retaking until it is right.

**THE LIVE GALLERY IS THE SOURCE OF TRUTH, NOT THIS FILE.** Aimee has shot and
uploaded a set organised by tab — Dashboard, Loot, Raiders, Calendar, Bosses,
Keys — and added the Raiders shots to the project by hand on 2026-08-25. The
files are under `screenshots/`. What is left below is the backlog: the shots
that have not been taken, and the notes on how to take them.

**A GALLERY IS NOT DOCUMENTATION.** Aimee, turning down a reshoot of the
settings window after it was rebuilt: "i dont think we need an updated settings
screenshot. thats not what makes someone download the addon." Every shot has to
earn its slot by selling the thing. Screens that answer questions people only
ask after installing belong in the description text, not in the pictures.

## Taking them

- **Print Screen** saves a shot to
  `World of Warcraft/_retail_/Screenshots/`.
- Set quality first: **Escape → Options → System → Advanced**, screenshot
  quality to maximum and format to **JPEG** (PNG files are large enough that
  the upload will be slow).
- Do **not** use the hide-interface key. It hides the addon too.
- Take them at your normal UI scale. A shot taken at an unusual scale looks
  wrong to everyone whose scale is different.

## Before you shoot

- `/syl theme` until it is on **Nightfall**, so every shot matches. Mixed
  color schemes across a gallery reads as inconsistency rather than as a
  feature. There is one deliberate exception, shot 9.
- Move the window somewhere with a plain background behind it. A busy raid
  encounter behind a data window makes the data unreadable.
- Turn off any addon that overlaps, if one does.

## Ready now

**1 — The main window, Drops tab.** `/syl`

The headline shot. Boss, item, winner, roll and win type all visible in one
frame. Make sure at least one **Need** and one **Transmog** win are on screen
together — that single frame is the whole argument for the addon, because it
shows the two being counted apart.

**2 — Filters in use.** `/syl`, then set a filter

Open the **Win type** dropdown with Transmog ticked, so the list visibly
narrows. Shows the filtering exists without needing a caption.

**3 — A drop's detail.** `/syl`, click any row

The full roll list: everyone who was eligible and what each of them chose.
Pick a drop with a lot of rollers — this is the data no other tracker keeps.

**4 — Player statistics.** `/syl players`

Eligible, wins, upgrades, transmog and drought side by side. Sort by drought
before shooting so the top row means something.

**5 — Chat Loot tab.** `/syl`, Chat Loot tab

Shows the volume being handled — a couple of hundred rows scrolling. Proves
it is not a toy.

**6 — Settings.** ~~`/syl settings`~~ **Dropped 2026-08-25.** There is a shot
on the page already and it does not need retaking after the five-tab redesign,
for the reason at the top of this file: nobody installs an addon because of its
options screen. The "can I turn things off" question is answered in the
description text, where it costs no gallery slot.

**7 — The minimap command menu.** ~~Right-click the minimap button~~
**Gone with the feature, 2026-08-25.** The menu was deleted — every command is
a button on Settings → Tools, and the minimap button's right-click drags the
icon now. Do not shoot this; it does not exist.

## After one real raid night

These four have no data yet. Shooting them now would produce four pictures of
an empty table, which reads as a broken addon rather than a new one.

**8 — Raid Nights.** `/syl raids`

Instance, difficulty, pulls, kills, raiders, length. Needs at least one night
with real pulls. Two or three nights makes a far better shot than one.

**9 — Boss History.** `/syl bosses`

Wants a night with both kills and wipes, so the kills column shows something
like `3/7` rather than a dash. **Take this one in Parchment** — `/syl theme
parchment` — as the single shot that shows the addon is not locked to one
look. Switch back afterwards.

**10 — The end of night summary in chat.** Leave the instance after a raid

The chat output: instance, duration, kills, drops, upgrades, and how many
people went home without an upgrade. Crop to the chat frame. This is the
moment the addon does something nobody asked it to, which is what gets
remembered.

Bonus if you catch it: `/syl due` right after a raid, ranking who has gone
longest without an upgrade. That is the feature that sells this to an officer,
and it is meaningless without attendance data behind it.

## Order on the page

1, 3, 4, 10, 2, 8, 9, 5.

Lead with what it records, then what it answers, then the interface. Nobody
scrolls a gallery to the end, which is the same reason 6 and 7 are struck out
above rather than moved to the back: a slot that has to go last is a slot that
was not worth having.
